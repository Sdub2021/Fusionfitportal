Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

FIT (fusionfitportal.com) is a movement and wellness app. This page explains what we collect and why. We keep this short on purpose.

What we collect

  • Email address, if you join the list.
  • Solana wallet address, if you add one.
  • Basic technical data the browser or phone already sends (for example page loads). We do not sell this.

The vestibular camera drill, the Yoga prayer-pose check, and the Tai Chi form check use your device camera on the device only. Face, hand, and body video is not uploaded or stored by FIT. Yoga and Tai Chi load Google’s on-device Hand Landmarker (full) in the browser.

The Tai Chi page can play a public YouTube reference (Cheng Man-Ching 37). That player is YouTube / Google, not FIT. We cannot read the video pixels. We only read your pose on this device and match it to the named move at that timestamp.

We do not ask for your seed phrase or private keys. Never type those into FIT.

Why we collect it

Email and wallet are stored so we can contact you about FIT and a possible future CMC37 token. Joining the list does not send tokens today. It only saves your details.

Where it is stored

Sign-up details are stored with Supabase, a third-party database host. They process data on our behalf. Do not use FIT if you are not comfortable with that.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your email or wallet address.
  • We do not use your data for medical diagnosis.
  • We do not take custody of your crypto.

How long we keep it

We keep list sign-ups until you ask us to delete them, or until the project no longer needs the list.

Your choices

You can use the practice parts of FIT without joining the list. To delete your sign-up, email us from the same address you used and include the wallet you submitted.
