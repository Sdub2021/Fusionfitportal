Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

FIT (“we”, “us”) operates fusionfitportal.com and the FIT mobile app listed on the Solana dApp Store. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Email address if you submit a claim or signup form.
  • Public Solana wallet address if you connect a wallet or claim CMC37.
  • Optional device label (for example “Seeker”) if provided by the app.
  • Technical data such as browser type and basic logs needed to run the site.

We do not collect seed phrases, private keys, or biometric data. Keys stay in your wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Seed Vault). Biometric approval happens on your device, not on our servers.

How we use it

  • To record claims and communicate about FIT / CMC37.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.
  • To comply with law if required.

We do not sell your personal information.

Wallets and blockchain

Wallet connections use Mobile Wallet Adapter or a browser wallet you choose. Transactions you approve are public on Solana. We cannot reverse on-chain activity.

Cookies

The site may use essential cookies or local storage to remember a session or PWA install. We do not use advertising trackers.

Third parties

Signup data may be stored with our database provider (Supabase). Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Those providers process data under their own terms.

Retention

We keep claim records as long as needed to operate FIT and honor entitlements, unless you ask us to delete what we can legally delete.

Your choices

You may disconnect your wallet at any time. To request deletion of an email/wallet signup we hold off-chain, contact us using the email below. On-chain records cannot be erased.

Children

FIT is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information for a child.

Contact

Questions: use the contact form on fusionfitportal.com or email the address associated with your claim.
